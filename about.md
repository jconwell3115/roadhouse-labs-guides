**Roadhouse Labs** is the personal brand and publishing home of an engineer whose
background spans two different environments: earlier work as a **Senior Network
Engineer at NASA**, and current work as a **Senior Network DevOps Engineer** at an
international organization operating across **10,000+ sites**.

The name reflects the ethos: a roadhouse is a place built for function, not
formality — useful, direct, and open to anyone who shows up. That's how these
guides are written.

These guides document the infrastructure I actually run — real configs, real
failure modes, and real fixes. Nothing here is a toy example or a simplified
illustration. Where there's a gotcha I hit, it's documented. Where there's a
decision that could go either way, I explain the tradeoff.

The focus is on self-hosting with modern Linux tooling: Podman, systemd quadlets,
firewalld, and the principle that your home network deserves the same rigor as
production infrastructure.
